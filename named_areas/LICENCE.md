# Boundary data licences

The repository's MIT licence covers its code. It does not replace the boundary publishers' terms.

## Europe

Source: Eurostat GISCO, LAU 2024 and NUTS 2024.

© EuroGeographics for the administrative boundaries

These boundaries are subject to [Eurostat's GISCO download provisions](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/gisco/geodata/statistical-units), including their non-commercial-use condition and attribution requirements. Commercial users must contact EuroGeographics. They are not offered here under MIT or an unrestricted open-data licence.

Outlines have been simplified and grid values aggregated by this project. Eurostat is not responsible for these changes or derived totals.

Country totals follow the published GISCO outline's footprint. Where the existing grids use separate owners, the report lists each owner and its contribution: France includes FRA, GLP, MTQ, GUF, REU, MYT and MAF (French Saint Martin); Norway includes NOR and SJM; Cyprus includes CYP and ZNC. This describes the input geometry and accounting, without changing any grid cell's ownership or taking a position on territorial status. Åland is already included in FIN in the existing grid. GISCO's Kosovo code XK maps to the existing grid owner KSV.

## United States

Source: US Census Bureau, 2025 cartographic boundary files, 1:500,000, places, counties and states. The country outline is the union of the 50 state and District of Columbia outlines; US territories are excluded.

Census TIGER products, including cartographic boundary shapefiles, are [in the public domain](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/archives/2014-pr/cb14-208.html).

## Derived totals

The totals inherit the source grid layers' data terms, including OpenStreetMap's ODbL for transit stops. See the repository's [methodology and source credits](https://github.com/joshp123/moneymap/blob/main/docs/methodology.md#credits-and-licences). Boundary geometry and the derived totals have distinct source licences.
